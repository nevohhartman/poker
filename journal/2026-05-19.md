Ok I have devised my method for storing the relevent poker hands:
Each hand will be given a 1 + 2 + 2 = 5 digit number as a score.
The first digit will store what ype of hand it is (there are 10 hands so it goes from 0 - 9).
Then next two digits store the first card high (2 is 02, ace is 14)
The next two digits stores the second card high (only required for full house)
