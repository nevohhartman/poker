The implentation for outs counting was interesting:
I had to find every card in the remaining deck(pretending the other players dont exist) such that it improved my hands score by a whole hand type. To avoid checking against my entire list of cards every time to see if it was one of the cards I had taken out, i simply gave each card a new boolean outs check, whihc is defaulted to false. Then we pass through your hand + centre once, setting it to true. Then pass through the deck, if it's not had out check, you run hand eval with this new card and see if score increases, if yes increase count. At the end you then have to set all the players + centre cards back to false.
